About & credits

A little archive of Jorvik

HM 2018 is an unofficial fan-made database for exploring Star Stable items and horses from the 2018 era, organising a collection and planning outfits.

Historical details are a work in progress. Unverified or unavailable entries are marked separately; prices, shops and stats should be checked against the version of the game you play.

Item pictures use Velausc’s SSO Items List. Horse pictures and database references include Star Stable Database. Data has also been imported from the site owner’s compiled archive.
