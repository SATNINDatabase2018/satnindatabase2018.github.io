About & credits

A little archive of Jorvik

I’m SATNIN, and I created SATNIN's Database for Horse Museum 2018 Version. My Horse Museum name is Luna Dovegarden (2018 version). I made this database to help you explore items and horses, organise your collection, and plan outfits.

I’m still working on the information, so some details may be incorrect. I’ve marked unverified or unavailable entries separately. If you notice a mistake in a price, shop or stat, please let me know.

The work behind my database

I’m SATNIN, and I’ve done the work that makes this database possible: extracting data, compiling and organising the records, comparing sources, and bringing the information together for this website. This has taken a lot of time and care, and I’m still working on corrections and missing details.

If you share or use my compiled database, please credit me as SATNIN. The original game assets and the external resources listed below belong to their respective creators.
