Unlocking areas

Unlocking Epona 2016–2020

To receive Herman’s message and begin the Epona entrance quests, you need to meet the level, reputation and quest requirements below.

Level requirement

You must be at least level 14. The reputation quests take time, so many players reach around level 16 before gaining access.

Reputation requirements

Reach at least Friendly with each of these factions:

  • GED Jarlaheim Office — Ms. Drake
  • Jorvik Stables
  • The Goldspur Family
  • The Jarlasson Family

Required questlines

  • Harvest Counties bridge: rebuild the bridge connecting Moorland to the Harvest Counties.
  • Jarlaheim’s mayor: help Jack Goldspur resolve the town’s mayoral problems.
  • Gretchen and the German family: find Gretchen around Fort Pinta and Hollow Woods, then progress through her family’s quests.
  • The summerhouse scam: complete the “Newcomer” story, helping Helga and her family after Mr. Kembell’s summerhouse scam.
  • Dino Valley: meet Nic Stoneground and help establish his camp in the Valley of the Hidden Dinosaur.
  • Ms. Drake’s reports: complete her GED tasks through “A step up in your career?”, then deliver her report to Herman.

Entering Epona

  1. Herman’s message: once all requirements are met, Herman sends an urgent text asking you to meet him at Jorvik Stables.
  2. Operation Grim Harvest: Herman explains GED’s expansion plans and asks you to find a way past them.
  3. The compass: work with the Goldspur family to obtain a special compass and locate a hidden route.
  4. The secret path: follow the trail through the Graydew Mountains to reach Epona.

Unlocking the Kallters 2016–2018

Meeting the Kallters requires several days of progression in the Valley of the Hidden Dinosaur.

Finding the Kallters

  1. Enter Dino Valley: reach at least level 15 and complete the quests involving Nic Stoneground’s crashed hot-air balloon to open the valley.
  2. Help the expedition: continue Nic Stoneground’s and Professor Einstein’s exploration and camp quests over several days.
  3. Discover the Icengate: after roughly three days of questing, follow the quest leading along the Hidden Road to the frozen gate.
  4. Meet Nanook and Sedna: the Kallters’ outer expedition is waiting outside the Icengate.

Building reputation

After meeting the Kallters, complete their daily tasks to build trust from Neutral towards Popular. Tasks include collecting call stones, ice, thistles, ice berries, ice chestnuts and snow weasels.

Rewards

Reaching the required reputation unlocks their exclusive purchases, including the Generation 1 Fjord horses sold outside the Icengate.
