How to use my database

Hi, I’m SATNIN! Here’s how to find items, keep track of your collection, and put together your favourite looks for Horse Museum 2018.

01

Start here

You can explore my database without an account. Create an account when you want to keep a collection, save horses, or make outfits.

  1. Open Database to browse.
  2. For personal features, open Account. Choose Continue with Discord to sign up or log in with Discord, or create an email-and-password account.
  3. If you choose email signup, check your inbox and spam folder for an email from Supabase Auth, confirm your email address, then log in with your email and password.

If you already have an account, you can log in first and choose Link with Discord on the Account page. This connects Discord to the account you’re using, including when your Discord email differs. It does not merge two accounts that already exist.

Discord sign-in does not require a separate website confirmation email. If you already have an email account here, use the same verified email on Discord; different email addresses may create separate accounts.

When you’re logged out, personal tools are blurred. Clicking an account-only action will show you how to log in or create an account.

02

Find an item or horse

Use the tabs beneath Database to switch between the different parts of the catalogue.

  1. Search for a title or use Category to choose a main group.
  2. Choose an Item type beneath it, such as saddles or tops. For horses, choose a breed and, if you like, a generation.
  3. Narrow the location with Area, then Site / shop. The shop list follows the items you’re filtering.

You can also click an item’s type bubble or its shop to browse that type or shop. As you scroll, a compact search and filter bar stays beneath the header.

03

Sort the results

Sorting changes the order of results. Filters decide which results appear.

Choose Sort by and a direction. Use Add sort for another rule: for example, Category first and Name second. The first rule takes priority; later rules break ties.

You can sort by name, SC, JS, category, location, generation, or stats. Overall stats (combined) adds the known stats together; choose descending to see the highest totals first. Individual stat choices change with the category.

Rewards and gifts appear after regular items. Unverified, unknown, or unavailable entries are tinted gray and stay at the end, regardless of sorting. A star on a pet means it can run beside you; hover over the star for the explanation.

04

Keep your collection

Use the buttons on an item to record what you own and what you’re looking for.

  1. Click I own this, Wishlist, or Favorite. Highlighted buttons show your selections.
  2. Open My Collection and switch between your owned items, wishlist, and favorites.
  3. Use the category and item-type filters to find something in your collection.

Click a selected item button again to remove that mark. The same item can be both owned and a favorite.

05

Build your stable

Add the horses you own and personalise them.

  1. Find a horse in Database and click Add to my stable. Enter a name when asked.
  2. In My Stable, choose its level or use Rename horse to change its name.
  3. Sort your horses by breed, level, or name.
  4. Choose saved outfits for a horse and click Save outfits.

Use Remove horse to remove a horse from your stable. Read the confirmation before continuing.

06

Create and save outfits

Make horse equipment sets, player outfits, or a combined look.

  1. In Database, click Add to outfit on an item. Tick one or more named outfits, or choose Create new outfit, then save your selections.
  2. Open My Outfits. Choose an outfit or click New outfit, give it a name, and choose its type.
  3. Click a labelled equipment or clothing slot to choose an item. Search the picker or use Only items I own. Saddle bags and pets have their own slots.
  4. For horse looks, choose a horse from your stable. Review the displayed stat totals, then click Save outfit.

New outfits created from a database item start with the matching type. Adding items from both sides makes a combined set. You can also change Outfit type or use Add horse equipment / Add player outfit. Removing a section asks for confirmation.

Use Combine outfits to join a saved horse-only set and a saved player-only set into a new combined outfit. Browse your saved looks below the creator, and click Open outfit to edit one. Remember to save your changes to your account.

07

Share your look

Show someone an outfit without giving them access to your account.

  1. Click Share outfit in the creator or on a saved outfit.
  2. Copy the generated link and send it to someone.

Anyone with the link can view that snapshot without logging in. Later edits or deleting the original outfit won’t change an existing shared link. Share only looks and horse names you’re happy for others to see.

08

Themes, corrections, and help

Make the website comfortable to use, and help me keep the information accurate.

Use the theme control to switch between cherry blossom and night. Your choice is remembered in your browser.

If you spot an incorrect entry or a website problem, message me on Discord: crabatthequeen. Include the item title, a picture if possible, and what needs correcting. Please don’t send passwords or private account details.
