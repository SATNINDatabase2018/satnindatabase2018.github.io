How to use my database

Hi, I’m SATNIN! Here’s how to find items, track your collection, and plan your looks for Horse Museum 2018.

01

Start here

You can browse Database, news, and guides without an account. Log in to use My Collection, My Wardrobe, My Stable, and My Outfits. Your saved items and wardrobe belong to your account and are available when you log in on another device.

  1. Open Account and choose Continue with Discord, or create an account with your email and a password.
  2. Email confirmation is no longer required for new signups. If signup logs you in, you can start using your account straight away.
  3. To add Discord to an existing account, log in first and choose Link with Discord on the Account page. Linking does not merge two existing accounts.

Click My Stable or My Collection in the header to open the page. Hover to see their section menus; on a touchscreen, use the arrow to open the menu. Personal features require you to log in.

02

Find an item or horse

Use the Database tabs to browse hairstyles, player clothes, player accessories, horse equipment, horses, horse decorations, or pets.

  1. Search names, descriptions, locations, stats, prices, or special features such as pony, cold-tolerant, and companion.
  2. In Database, you can select multiple Categories, Item types, Areas, and Sites / shops. Choices within one filter match any selected value; different filters work together.
  3. The item-type list follows your selected categories, and the shop list follows your areas. With multiple categories chosen, types from all of them are included. Horses use breeds as their item types.

For Hairstyles, the type filter is called Hairstyle and includes general hairstyle pictures. Individual cards show the colour variant; the description also includes the general picture when available.

Search results prioritise name → description → site/shop → stats → price → special features. Unverified entries stay at the bottom even when searching. Items with several shops can match any of their listed locations.

My items (match any selected) shows items with any selected mark: I own this, Wishlist, or Favorite. Don’t show (any selected) excludes items with any selected mark. These filters also work for horses: ownership means you have that horse in My Stable.

Use Load 60 more to expand the results. Marking an item or adding it to an outfit keeps your expanded list open.

03

Sort the results

Choose a sort field and direction. Use + Add sorting rule for another rule, such as Item type followed by Name. Search relevance takes priority when searching; sort rules break ties. Otherwise, the first sort rule takes priority.

Options include name, SC, JS, location, item type, and relevant stats. Equipment’s speed stat is called Swiftness. Overall stats (combined) adds the known stats; choose descending for the highest totals first. For Hairstyles, the options are Name, SC, Location, Hair color, and Hairstyle.

A compact toolbar appears as you scroll through Database, My Collection, and My Stable. Use All filters & sorts to return to the main controls and Reset filters & sorting to start again.

04

Keep your collection

On an item’s card, click I own this, Wishlist, or Favorite. Click again to remove the mark. An item can have several marks at once. Badges and theme-matched colours help you see its status.

Open My Collection or use its header dropdown:

  • Everything: all your owned, wishlisted, and favorited items together, without duplicates.
  • Owned: items marked I own this.
  • Wishlist: items you want.
  • Favorites: your favorite items.
  • Wardrobe (My Storage): your own storage layout.

Use the collection’s search and filters to narrow the results. Horses are managed separately in My Stable, including its Wishlist tab.

05

Find your things with My Wardrobe

Use My Collection → Wardrobe (My Storage) to record where your items are stored in the game. Arrange them to match your in-game wardrobe, then search here when you need to find something. You arrange the items yourself; the website does not sync with the game.

  1. Mark items I own this in Database. Only owned items can be organised.
  2. Choose Clothes for player clothes and accessories, Horse gear for equipment and horse decorations, or Pets for pets.
  3. Choose a numbered page. Clothes and Horse gear each have 15 pages; Pets has 7. Use Name this page to add a title while keeping its number.
  4. Click one or more items to select them; click a selected item again to deselect it. Click an empty square to place the selection, or drag a selected item. Multiple items fill free squares from that position, skipping occupied ones. A move is rejected if there is not enough room.
  5. To take items out of a page, select them and choose Move to unsorted, or drag them back to the Unsorted panel. The final square on each page always stays blank.

Wardrobe search looks across all categories and pages, including Unsorted. Each result shows its storage page; click it to open that page. Numbered buttons also show matching-item counts in the current category while searching.

Hover over a page number for a preview of its contents. Hover over an item for its card and In your outfits, listing your saved outfits that contain it. Use Refresh wardrobe to reload your saved layout and outfit list after changes elsewhere.

The small star on an item tile lets you favorite or unfavorite it. The star in the hover preview only shows its favorite status.

Your layout saves automatically after successful moves and page renaming. Each account has its own private wardrobe; other users cannot view or edit it. Removing an item’s owned mark also removes it from your storage layout. Hairstyles are not stored in these wardrobe groups.

06

Build your stable

  1. Find a horse in Database, click Add to My Stable, and give it a name. You can add more than one of the same horse.
  2. Use Wishlist and Favorite on horse cards to mark horses you like. The card’s colour shows saved status.
  3. When you own a horse, its Database card shows your count beneath the add button. Click that count to open My Stable filtered to those horses.
  4. Open My Stable → My Horses to rename horses, set their levels, and attach saved outfits. Click Save outfits after changing outfit assignments.
  5. Choose Wishlist to see your wishlisted horses. Search or sort the list to find a horse.

Remove horse removes that individual horse from your stable after confirmation. It does not delete the horse from the public database.

07

Create and save outfits

  1. In Database, click Add to outfit. Tick one or more outfits, or create a new outfit, then save your selections.
  2. Open My Outfits. Choose an outfit or create a new one, give it a name, and choose horse equipment, a player outfit, or a combined set.
  3. Click an outfit slot to pick an item. Search or filter the picker, or use Only items I own. The player layout includes a Hairstyle slot beside Hat and a player accessories slot. Horse looks include decorations, saddle bag, and pet slots.
  4. Use the × on a selected item to remove it individually. Review the stats and click Save outfit to save your changes to your account.

Use Combine outfits to combine horse and player outfits into a new set. In Browse all outfits, hover over an outfit title to preview its layout and stats, or click Open outfit to edit it. Unsaved changes are labelled; remember to save them.

Outfit previews and totals keep zero stats visible. The totals show equipment bonuses, not your horse’s level, care, or base skills.

08

Share your look

Click Share outfit and copy the link. Anyone with that link can view the shared snapshot without logging in. They cannot edit your account. Later changes or deleting the original outfit do not change an existing snapshot, so share only information you are comfortable showing.

09

HM Guides

Open HM Guides in the header:

  • Spirit timetable: the current UTC day, hour, and location are highlighted while Spirit is available. You can also open it from Spirit’s Database card.
  • Championships: see the next race, its UTC day and time, and a countdown.
  • Unlocking areas: read the historical Epona and Kallters requirements.

Times are shown in UTC. Use the linked time converter to check your local time. Scroll sideways on smaller screens to see every day.

10

News, reactions, and banners

Open News from the footer for From SATNIN announcements, database edits, and the latest automatic website-change summary. Filter by update type or click Show comments. Admins publish news and comments; everyone can read them.

Signed-in users can add emoji reactions. Click an emoji again to remove your reaction. Everyone can see reaction counts.

The compact banner beneath the header shows the latest From SATNIN announcement across pages. Click × to dismiss that announcement, or Never show news banners again to hide future banners too. Signed-in choices are saved to your account across devices; logged-out choices are remembered separately in that browser.

11

Themes, corrections, and help

Use the theme selector for Blossom, Night, Coffee, Night Forest, Dark Coffee, or the Elvis-inspired TCB. The browser remembers your choice.

Close item and outfit dialogs with their close button, Escape, or a click outside the menu. Wardrobe item previews disappear when you leave the item so you can reach neighboring tiles.

If an entry looks incorrect or something does not work, contact me on Discord: crabatthequeen. Include the item title, what happened, and a picture if helpful. Never send passwords or private account details.
