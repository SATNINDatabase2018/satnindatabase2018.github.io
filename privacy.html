Privacy

Accounts and saved information

The public database can be browsed without an account. Account features use Supabase for authentication and storage. If you create an account, the site uses your email address and any username you provide. Passwords are handled by the authentication provider.

Your account can store collection, wishlist and favourite selections, outfits, and horses you add to your stable, including their names, nicknames, levels and outfit assignments.

Storage in your browser

The site stores your selected colour theme and outfit drafts in browser storage. Authentication may also store session information so you can remain signed in. Clearing browser storage can remove local drafts and sign you out; it does not delete saved account data.

Hosting and external services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Authentication and account storage use Supabase. Item pictures, horse pictures and the Supabase client script may load from external providers, including GitHub, Star Stable Database and jsDelivr. Your browser sends technical request information to those providers when loading their resources.

See GitHub’s privacy statement and Supabase’s privacy policy for their practices.

Questions and account requests

To ask about your information or request correction or deletion of account data, contact the site owner through the Contact page. Never include your password in a request.
